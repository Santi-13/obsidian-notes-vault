#Chairing #Definition 
The ***Frobenius Norm*** of a matrix $A \in \mathrm{R}^{n \times n}$, denoted as $\lvert\lvert A \rvert\rvert_{F}$, measures its "magnitude" and is defined as:
$$
\lvert\lvert A \rvert\rvert_{F} = \sqrt{ \sum^m_{i=1} \sum^n_{j=1} \lvert A_{ij} \rvert^2   } 
$$
As we can see, it is the square root of the sum of the square of its elements. For example, for:

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4 
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
\lvert\lvert A \rvert\rvert_{F} = \sqrt{ 1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{ 30 }
$$
