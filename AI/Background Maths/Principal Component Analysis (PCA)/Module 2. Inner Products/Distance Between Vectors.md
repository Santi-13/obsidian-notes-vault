The ***distance*** between two *vectors* is defined as the **length** of the *difference vector*. If we use a [[AI/Background Maths/Principal Component Analysis (PCA)/Module 2. Inner Products/Dot Product|Dot Product]] , then the distance is called the **Euclidean distance**.
$$
d(x,y) = || x-y || = \sqrt{ \langle x-y, x-y \rangle }
$$
