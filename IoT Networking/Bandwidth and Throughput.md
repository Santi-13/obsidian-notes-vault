#Networking #NetworkingBasics
##### *Networking Basics*
---
**Bandwidth** is the **capacity of a medium to carry data**. **Digital bandwidth** measures the amount of data that can flow from one place to another in a given amount of time. It's typically measured in the number of **bits** that (theoretically) can be sent across the media in a second. Common measurements are:
- **Thousands of bits per second (Kbps)**
- **Millions of bits per second (Mbps)**
- **Billions of bits per second (Gbps)**

**Throughput** is the **actual amount of data** that successfully transfers, and it **does not usually match the specified bandwidth**. Factors that influence throughput include:
- The **amount of data** being sent and received over the connection.
- The **latency** created by the number of network devices encountered between source and destination.    

**Latency** refers to the **amount of time, including delays**, for data to travel from one given point to another.
