#### Personal Area Network (PAN) -
Small network of wirelessly connected devices within personal reach. For example, Bluetooth or other less-known connection protocols such as Zigbee and Ultra-Wide Band (UWB).

#### Local Area Network (LAN) -
Networks connecting at least to devices typically restricted to a small geographic area, such as a home, a business or similar. They provide larger access to WANs
