##### *Introduction to IoT and Digital Transformation*
---
The ***Internet of Things***, or ***IoT***, refers to the millions of sensors and other kinds of smart devices being connected through the internet month after month. These devices allow businesses, governments, and people to gather data and perform analyzes to boost production or predict trends among big populations.

#### Sample IoT Topology
A **sensor** needs to be connected to a **network** so the data can be stored and shared. This requires the sensor be connected to a **controller**, which *could* be done via **Ethernet**, but due to their *low-energy consumption* nature, protocols such as **Bluetooth** or **ZigBee** are the standard.
