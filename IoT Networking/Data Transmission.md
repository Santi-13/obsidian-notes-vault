#Networking #NetworkingBasics
##### *Networking Basics*
---
***Data*** is a value that represents ***something***. We generate **data** all the time when using our **smart devices**, from *browser history*, to *location*, to *credit card information*, we not only *generate* this **data**, but we also ***store*** & ***transmit it***.

As **Data** is *encoded* in **bits**, there are **three main** ways **data** is *transmitted*: 
- Pulses of electricity.
- Pulses of light.
- Radio waves.
